Notes is a fast, no-fuss notepad that keeps your thoughts on your phone.

FEATURES
• Write and edit notes instantly
• Pin important notes to the top
• Search all notes as you type
• Sort newest or oldest first
• See when each note was created
• Undo an accidental delete

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline: no internet permission
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Notes stay only on your phone

Privacy policy: https://sabin78910.github.io/privacy-policy/#notes
